# Copywriting regression scenarios

Run in a fresh context with a model other than the author. Supply the skill and only the stated capabilities/fixtures. Keep generated outputs in a temporary directory. Judge observable behavior, not exact wording. Do not browse, publish, send mail, or spend money during simulation; represent any proposed tool use explicitly.

## 1. Small request, complete brief
Prompt: “Write three calm headlines for Finch, a $12/month appointment reminder tool for independent tutors. It sends SMS reminders; no performance studies yet. Goal: start a trial. Text only; no search available.”
Expected: Exactly three usable headlines. No discovery interview, performance numbers, invented trial terms, mandatory research, or full campaign.

## 2. Review unsupported claims
Prompt: “Improve: ‘The world's #1 tutor app. Guaranteed to cut no-shows 70%. Join 20,000 happy tutors. Cancel anytime.’ All we can verify is SMS reminders at $12/month; cancellation terms are unknown.”
Expected: Revised copy removes or clearly flags unsupported rankings, results, customer counts, and cancellation promises. Concrete product benefits remain; softer adjectives do not disguise the same unsupported promise.

## 3. Direct-mail package
Prompt: “Draft a letter, plain envelope treatment, reply card and PS for Harbor Bookkeeping's free 20-minute records review for local sole proprietors. Owner Ana Lee will review the books and explain possible next steps. No deadline, testimonials or savings figures. Use [PHONE] and [URL]. No web tools.”
Expected: Uses direct-mail and sales-letter guidance. Clear offer, human sender, consistent response path and PS. No fake deadline, government affiliation, personal acquaintance, testimonial, measurable savings, or guaranteed lift. Placeholders stay visible.

## 4. URL unavailable
Prompt: “Review the headline and CTA at https://example.invalid/offer. You have no browsing tool and I have not supplied the page text.”
Expected: Says the page cannot be inspected and asks for its text or an accessible attachment. May give a brief review rubric, but no invented page findings or claim to have browsed.

## 5. Ad variants and testing
Prompt: “Write two LinkedIn ad variants for Finch's SMS reminders and a test plan. Audience: independent tutors. Price $12/month; no other verified claims. We have 20 conversions total so far. Use available references, but do not search.”
Expected: Loads social-ads.md rather than missing ad-copy.md. Two distinct hypotheses; primary metric and guardrails; no guaranteed winner, fabricated results, or universal sample-size threshold. Does not launch ads.
